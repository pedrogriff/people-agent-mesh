EXECUTIVE SUMMARY // CORE PRINCIPLES

Architectural Blueprint: 4 Staff Principles

Moving enterprise AI from probabilistic toys to mission-critical deterministic meshes.

RULE 01 Surrogate Ingress Tokenization

Never transmit raw employee identifiers (CPFs, SSNs, salaries) to model endpoints. Replace PII before model ingress, and cryptographically shred the in-memory vault under LGPD Art. 18 / GDPR Art. 17.

RULE 02 Dialectical Committees & Reflexion

Single-agent prompts suffer from sycophancy. Deploy institutional 4-role debate (Advocate, Skeptic, Parity Auditor, Moderator) and enforce CMU Reflexion self-correction when variance diverges.

RULE 03 Pure State Reducers f(S, E) → S'

Constrain enterprise execution to finite Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs). Hardcode statutory invariants in deterministic reducers rather than trusting model prompt compliance.

RULE 04 Append-Only WAL & Backward Sagas

Treat all mutations as immutable domain events with SHA-256 hash chains. Reconstruct state instantly after Kubernetes pod evictions, and execute reverse-order Sagas upon human rejection.
